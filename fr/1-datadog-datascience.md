Applied Science Intern
Paris, France

As an Applied Science Intern, you will join a cross-functional team of engineers and applied scientists building AI-powered features within the Datadog platform. You will contribute to both existing and new products by improving machine learning models, developing new AI capabilities, and helping bring research into production. Working closely with engineering and product partners, you'll gain hands-on experience deploying AI systems at scale while strengthening your product mindset, collaboration, and communication skills. You'll also have opportunities to contribute to cutting-edge research that directly powers customer-facing features.

At Datadog, we place value in our office culture - the relationships and collaboration it builds and the creativity it brings to the table. We operate as a hybrid workplace to ensure our Datadogs can create a work-life harmony that best fits them.

 

What You’ll Do:

Design solutions for AI use cases by researching and benchmarking state-of-the-art models to identify the best approach
Build highly scalable product features using generative AI, machine learning algorithms, and statistical techniques
Contribute to the development, deployment, monitoring, and maintenance of production AI models, services, and infrastructure
Explore, analyze, and derive insights from the large-scale observability data flowing through Datadog systems
Participate in technical knowledge sharing by presenting recent academic research and investigating emerging model architectures to improve existing and future products
Collaborate with engineering and product teams to productionize AI solutions and deliver customer-facing features
 

Who You Are:

You will graduate in 2027 with a degree in Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Statistics, Computer Science, or a related field
You have experience building machine learning models through coursework, research, internships, or personal projects
You have strong programming skills and are eager to learn new languages and technologies
You communicate complex technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences
You have a strong interest in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data-driven product development
Available to complete a 6-month internship beginning in February, March, April, May, or June 2027 (we are unable to accommodate internships starting at any other time)
Datadog values people from all walks of life. We know not everyone will meet all the above qualifications on day one. That’s okay. If you’re passionate about technology and want to grow your experience, we encourage you to apply.

 

Benefits and Growth:

Work on meaningful technical challenges alongside engineers across the globe
Participate in mentorship and buddy programs designed to support learning and development
Join an in-person intern cohort, attend company social events, and engage with Datadog Community Guilds and employee resource groups throughout the internship
Receive relocation support and travel benefits where applicable
Enjoy office perks including snacks, drinks, and catered lunches on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday
Benefits and Growth listed above may vary based on the country of your employment and the nature of your employment with Datadog.

 

Please ensure that you submit your CV/resume in English. If possible, please apply using your personal email address instead of your university email address.

Come Grow with Us
We believe in continuous growth—across our people, our product, and our global footprint. That’s why it’s important to us that we invest heavily in both your personal and professional development. Through a focused approach we curate, develop, and deliver quality initiatives and programs that are accessible and useful to all Datadogs.

New Hire
Experience

We have comprehensive onboarding programs to help new joiners feel prepared, supported, and included through their first 90 days in their role. Each new-hire class learns the ins and outs of Datadog, from our product to our company culture.

Icon of charts and graphs on display.
Learning & Development Workshops

Datadogs can participate in a number of workshops throughout the year to strengthen skills in communication, feedback, and career navigation. We routinely evaluate our offerings to ensure we meet employee demand and business needs.

Icon of one person and an upward pointing arrow.
Manager
Development

All of our people managers participate in programming to understand what is expected of them and to build a network of leaders across the business. We offer over a year’s worth of development activities, including ones for individual contributors who wish to explore a career path in management.

Global
Events

We regularly host events that provide Datadogs dedicated time to focus on their personal growth and development. Our most popular event initiative to date is Learning Week, a biannual week-long event that promotes employee-to-employee knowledge sharing across the company.

Inclusion

We host several Inclusion-specific workshops to upskill Datadogs on topics like bias, behaviors of inclusion, and allyship.

Digital Learning
Platforms

We offer a number of different software licenses that provide access to online learning content anytime, anywhere. We continuously evaluate our tech stack and course catalog to ensure our material stays relevant and accessible.
